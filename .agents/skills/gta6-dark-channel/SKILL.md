# GTA 6 Dark Channel Production Guide (PS5 Gameplay)

This skill provides step-by-step templates, video layout setups, and viral scripting formulas for building a faceless (dark) YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels channel around GTA 6 using PS5 recorded gameplay.

---

## 1. PS5 Capture Optimization

* **Format**: Ensure PS5 records in **MP4** (`Settings > Captures and Broadcasts > Video Clip Format > File Type: MP4`).
* **Resolution**: 1080p60 or 4K60.
* **Transfer**: Copy files via USB 3.0 / SSD into `data/cache/` or upload via the web interface.

---

## 2. Recommended Video Layout in MyCliping

Since faceless gameplay footage does not contain a speaker facecam:
* **Primary Layout**: `blur_bg` (Blurred Ambient Background).
  - Keeps the 16:9 widescreen gameplay sharp and centered in 1080x608.
  - Dynamically blurs and expands the top and bottom with the game's vibrant Vice City / neon colors.
* **Subtitle Preset**:
  - Style: `cyber_neon` (Cyan/Pink accents) or `fire_flame` (Yellow/Orange accents).
  - Hook Title at the top with high emotional curiosity.

## 3. High-Retention Script Templates (Shorts/TikTok < 50s)

### Template A: "Did You Notice This Detail?" (Curiosity Hook)
1. **[0s - 3s] Hook**: "Quase ninguém percebeu o que a Rockstar fez com a física desse detalhe em GTA 6..."
2. **[3s - 25s] The Demonstration**: Show the PS5 gameplay moment with zoom effect or slow motion on the key action.
3. **[25s - 40s] Context / Comparison**: Compare with GTA 5 or real-life Miami footage.
4. **[40s - 48s] Call to Action (CTA)**: "Você acha que isso vai mudar a gameplay? Comente o que você achou e se inscreva!"

### Template B: "Myth / Test" (Interactive Hook)
1. **[0s - 3s] Hook**: "Será que a polícia do GTA 6 realmente se lembra do seu carro?"
2. **[3s - 30s] Execution**: Show the test step-by-step with dramatic game audio and energetic subtitles.
3. **[30s - 45s] The Result**: The unexpected or hilarious outcome.
4. **[45s - 50s] CTA**: "Qual o próximo mito que devemos testar no PS5? Deixe nos comentários!"
